Built for Society Committees & Residents

Everything needed for transparent maintenance accounting and hassle-free bill settlement.

For Society Committees (Admins)

  • Generate monthly maintenance bills with clear charge heads (maintenance, sinking fund, repair & maintenance fund, non-occupancy surcharge, and late interest).
  • Send WhatsApp bill notices with resident, flat, amount, and due-date details.
  • Track pending and paid invoices across flats and wings with clear audit logs.
  • Manage society onboarding and subscription plans smoothly.

For Residents

  • View pending dues across single or multiple flats and societies in one place.
  • Expand any bill to see itemized charges, late penalties, and applied wallet credits.
  • Pay single bills or consolidated society dues online via UPI, cards, or netbanking.
  • Download and share payment receipts anytime.

Refer & Earn: ₹1 Credit Per Billable Flat

Share your referral code with neighboring housing society committees. When a referred society starts its subscription, earn ₹1 credit per billable flat—applied toward subscription renewals for admins or maintenance dues for residents.
