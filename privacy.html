Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SettlePay (https://settlepay.co.in/ and the SettlePay application) collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact & Account Info: Mobile phone numbers, names, email addresses (where provided), and role (Admin or Resident).
  • Society & Billing Data: Society details, flat/unit numbers, occupancy/unit metadata, maintenance invoices, due dates, payment statuses, wallet credits, and referral records.
  • Transaction & Diagnostic Info: Order/payment reference IDs, payment mode/channel details, timestamps, and technical logs required to operate and secure the service. Sensitive payment credentials (such as card numbers, UPI PINs, or bank passwords) are handled by the payment gateway and are not stored as raw credentials by SettlePay.

2. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate users and send requested WhatsApp messages (such as login OTPs and bill notices).
  • To generate, display, and reconcile maintenance bills, receipts, and subscription statuses.
  • To track referral eligibility and wallet credit balances.
  • To maintain security, prevent duplicate settlements, troubleshoot issues, and comply with applicable law.

3. Sharing, Retention & Security

Relevant unit and billing details are shared within a society's admin and resident workflows, and with infrastructure, messaging, and payment service providers strictly as needed to provide the service. We retain information as needed for service delivery, accounting/audit trails, and legal compliance, and use reasonable technical measures to protect data. Contact us for assistance with profile corrections or data inquiries.
