Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: November 19, 2026

1. Society Maintenance Bill Payments (Resident Dues)

  • Maintenance dues paid by residents are applied toward invoices issued by their housing society.
  • Any refund, credit adjustment, or reversal of paid society maintenance dues is subject to approval by the respective housing society managing committee.
  • If a duplicate payment occurs due to a technical issue, please share the payment/order reference ID and receipt with your society admin and SettlePay support for verification.
  • If an amount is debited for a failed or incomplete transaction, banks/payment gateways generally auto-refund the amount within standard banking timelines (typically 5–7 business days).

2. SettlePay Platform Subscriptions (Society Admins)

  • Society admins may choose not to renew a subscription prior to the next billing cycle.
  • Unless agreed otherwise in writing, fees paid for an active subscription period (monthly or annual) are non-refundable for unused portions of that period.
  • For suspected duplicate or erroneous subscription charges, contact us within [7 days] of the charge with your society name and payment reference ID.
